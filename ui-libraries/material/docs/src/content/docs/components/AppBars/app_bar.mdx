The top app bar family displays the screen title and navigation and action
icons at the top of the screen. All variants expose `leading-button` and
`trailing-buttons` slots, drive their colors from the `AppBarTokens` role
values, and support scroll-driven collapse through `scroll-position` and
`scroll-behavior`.

The `MediumTopAppBar` and `LargeTopAppBar` variants place the title in a
second row, `MediumFlexibleTopAppBar` and `LargeFlexibleTopAppBar`
additionally render a `subtitle`, and `TwoRowsTopAppBar` is the generic base
for custom two-row layouts.

## Scroll behavior

Bind `scroll-position` to `-flickable.content-y` of the scrolled content.
`exit-until-collapsed` shrinks the bar on scroll-down and re-expands when the
content is scrolled back to the top. `enter-always` consumes deltas in both
directions. The `height-offset`, `content-offset`, `collapsed-fraction` and
`overlapped-fraction` properties mirror the `TopAppBarState` of Compose
Material 3, and the container color cross-fades to
`scrolled-container-color` with the `FastOutLinearInEasing` curve. Releasing
a partial collapse snaps the bar with the active motion scheme's
default-effects spring (`TopAppBarDefaults.snapAnimationSpec`).

## Properties

### title
<SlintProperty propName="title" typeName="string">
The primary title text.
</SlintProperty>

### subtitle
<SlintProperty propName="subtitle" typeName="string">
A second line of text under the title (`MediumFlexibleTopAppBar`,
`LargeFlexibleTopAppBar`, `TwoRowsTopAppBar`).
</SlintProperty>

### leading-button
<SlintProperty propName="leading-button" typeName="struct" structName="IconButtonItem">
An icon button item displayed at the start of the app bar.
</SlintProperty>

### trailing-buttons
<SlintProperty propName="trailing-buttons" typeName="struct" structName="IconButtonItem">
Icon button items displayed at the end of the app bar.
</SlintProperty>

### scroll-behavior
<SlintProperty propName="scroll-behavior" typeName="enum" enumName="TopAppBarScrollBehavior">
`pinned`, `exit-until-collapsed`, or `enter-always`.
</SlintProperty>

### scroll-position
<SlintProperty propName="scroll-position" typeName="length">
The scrolled amount of the content; bind `-flickable.content-y`.
</SlintProperty>

### title-horizontal-alignment
<SlintProperty propName="title-horizontal-alignment" typeName="enum" enumName="TextHorizontalAlignment">
`start` or `center` (`CenterAlignedTopAppBar` presets `center`).
</SlintProperty>

## Callbacks

### leading-button-clicked()
Invoked when the leading icon is clicked.

### trailing-button-clicked(index)
Invoked when a trailing icon is clicked.
