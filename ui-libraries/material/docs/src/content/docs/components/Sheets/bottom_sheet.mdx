A `BottomSheet` is the standard, non-modal sheet: it docks to the bottom edge of its container and
coexists with the content behind it. The component fills its parent's bounds, which are the anchor
space for `hidden`, `partially-expanded` and `expanded`. Drag the sheet by its handle or call the
state functions to move between values; the motion uses the spatial springs for show/expand and the
effects springs for hide/partial-collapse.

`BottomSheetDragHandle` is the pill strip every sheet draws when `has-drag-handle` is set —
click it to toggle, or use it standalone for custom sheet chrome. It exposes `expand()`,
`collapse()` and `clicked()` for the accessibility actions.

## Properties

### container-color
<SlintProperty propName="container-color" typeName="color" defaultValue="SheetBottomTokens.docked_container_color">
The sheet surface's fill color.
</SlintProperty>

### elevation-level
<SlintProperty propName="elevation-level" typeName="int" defaultValue="0">
The tonal/shadow elevation level applied to the sheet surface — the standard sheet uses level 0.
</SlintProperty>

### gestures-enabled
<SlintProperty propName="gestures-enabled" typeName="bool" defaultValue="true">
Whether the sheet can be dragged between its anchors, including fling-with-release-velocity settle.
</SlintProperty>

### has-drag-handle
<SlintProperty propName="has-drag-handle" typeName="bool" defaultValue="true">
Whether the 48 dp drag-handle strip with the pill is drawn at the sheet's top edge.
</SlintProperty>

### initial-value
<SlintProperty propName="initial-value" typeName="enum" enumName="SheetValue" defaultValue="SheetValue.hidden">
The anchor the sheet starts on.
</SlintProperty>

### pane-title
<SlintProperty propName="pane-title" typeName="string" defaultValue="Bottom sheet">
The accessibility pane title announced for the sheet.
</SlintProperty>

### sheet-max-width
<SlintProperty propName="sheet-max-width" typeName="length" defaultValue="640px">
The width cap applied to the sheet surface — wider containers center the sheet.
</SlintProperty>

### sheet-shape
<SlintProperty propName="sheet-shape" typeName="enum" enumName="MaterialCornerShape" defaultValue="SheetBottomTokens.docked_container_shape">
The top-corner shape of the sheet surface (`corner-extra-large-top` by default).
</SlintProperty>

### skip-partially-expanded
<SlintProperty propName="skip-partially-expanded" typeName="bool" defaultValue="false">
Removes the `partially-expanded` anchor — drags and programmatic moves settle directly between
`hidden` and `expanded`.
</SlintProperty>

### sheet-offset
<SlintProperty propName="sheet-offset" typeName="length" propertyVisibility="in-out">
The surface's current top edge in container coordinates — unclamped while a spring is in flight,
so it may overshoot past an anchor; `clamped-offset` is the bounded form.
</SlintProperty>

## State

### anchor-current
<SlintProperty propName="anchor-current" typeName="enum" enumName="SheetValue" propertyVisibility="out">
The anchor the surface currently occupies — `current-value` is the settle target, this tracks
where the surface actually rests while a spring is in flight.
</SlintProperty>

### current-value / target-value
<SlintProperty propName="current-value" typeName="enum" enumName="SheetValue" propertyVisibility="out">
The value the sheet is settling to and the value it is currently animating toward.
</SlintProperty>

### is-visible
<SlintProperty propName="is-visible" typeName="bool" propertyVisibility="out">
Whether the surface has left the `hidden` anchor.
</SlintProperty>

### dragging
<SlintProperty propName="dragging" typeName="bool" propertyVisibility="out">
Whether a pointer is currently dragging the sheet.
</SlintProperty>

### has-expanded-state / has-partially-expanded-state
<SlintProperty propName="has-expanded-state" typeName="bool" propertyVisibility="out">
Whether the corresponding anchor is available in the current container.
</SlintProperty>

### sheet-height / surface-x / surface-y / surface-width / surface-height
<SlintProperty propName="sheet-height" typeName="length" propertyVisibility="out">
The measured sheet height and the surface's live geometry in container coordinates.
</SlintProperty>

## Functions

### show() / expand() / partial-expand() / hide()
Moves the sheet to the named anchor with the matching spring.

### snap-to(value: SheetValue)
Moves to `value` without velocity — the frame-accurate jump used for programmatic changes.

### animate-to-dismiss() / settle-to-dismiss()
Dismiss paths: `animate-to-dismiss()` plays the hide spring and reports `on-dismiss-request()`
when the surface reaches `hidden`; `settle-to-dismiss()` jumps immediately.

## Callbacks

### deny-value-change(value: SheetValue) -> bool
Return `true` to veto an anchor change — the upstream `confirmValueChange`.

### on-dismiss-request()
Invoked when the sheet completed its dismiss motion and asks to be removed.
