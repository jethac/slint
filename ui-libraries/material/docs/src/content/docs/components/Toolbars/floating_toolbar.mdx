`HorizontalFloatingToolbar` and `VerticalFloatingToolbar` are the Material 3
Expressive floating toolbars — a 64dp stadium of icon actions that floats
over content, optionally paired with a floating action button. Set
`fab-icon` for the with-FAB variant: the toolbar clips toward the FAB as it
collapses, and the FAB grows from 56dp to 80dp
(`FloatingToolbarTokens`/`FabBaselineTokens`/`FabMediumTokens`).

`color-style` selects the `standard` (`surface_container`) or `vibrant`
(`primary_container` toolbar, `tertiary_container` FAB) color pair.
`fab-position` places the FAB at `start`/`end` (horizontal) or
`top`/`bottom` (vertical).

Center content is arbitrary children — `IconButton`s in the example — or
use the `leading-items`/`trailing-items` `AppBarItem` slots.

## Scroll behavior

Two independent behaviors mirror
`FloatingToolbarDefaults.floatingToolbarVerticalNestedScroll` and
`exitAlwaysScrollBehavior`:

- `expand-on-scroll`: collapse after `collapse-scroll-distance-threshold`
  (40dp) of forward scroll, expand again after
  `expand-scroll-distance-threshold` (40dp) back.
- `exit-always`: slide the whole toolbar off its `exit-direction` edge with
  the scroll position.

Bind `scroll-position` to `-flickable.content-y` (plus
`scroll-release-velocity` to `flickable.release-velocity.y` and
`scroll-parent-extent` to the parent's `height`/`width` for `bottom`/`end`
exits); releasing a partial offset settles with the motion scheme's
fast-spatial spring. When `exit-always` is set, the bar itself is a drag
area: dragging it vertically drives the same offset and settles on release.
`collapsed-fraction` reports the current 0–1 collapse for chained effects.

## Properties

### leading-items / trailing-items
<SlintProperty propName="leading-items" typeName="struct" structName="AppBarItem">
`AppBarItem` slots around the center content (leading items first).
</SlintProperty>

### fab-icon
<SlintProperty propName="fab-icon" typeName="image">
FAB icon; when set the toolbar renders the with-FAB layout.
</SlintProperty>

### fab-position
<SlintProperty propName="fab-position" typeName="enum" enumName="FloatingToolbarHorizontalFabPosition">
FAB placement — `start`/`end` on `HorizontalFloatingToolbar`,
`FloatingToolbarVerticalFabPosition.top`/`bottom` on the vertical variant.
</SlintProperty>

### expanded
<SlintProperty propName="expanded" typeName="bool">
The toolbar's expanded state — the `expand-on-scroll` hysteresis writes it
back, so bind it `<=>` when the caller tracks it.
</SlintProperty>

### color-style
<SlintProperty propName="color-style" typeName="enum" enumName="FloatingToolbarColorStyle">
`standard` or `vibrant` container/FAB color pair.
</SlintProperty>

### expand-on-scroll
<SlintProperty propName="expand-on-scroll" typeName="bool">
Enables the scroll-distance expand/collapse hysteresis.
</SlintProperty>

### exit-always
<SlintProperty propName="exit-always" type="bool">
Enables the scroll-driven exit in `exit-direction`.
</SlintProperty>

### exit-direction
<SlintProperty propName="exit-direction" typeName="enum" enumName="FloatingToolbarExitDirection">
Edge the bar exits toward — `bottom`, `top`, `start`, or `end`.
</SlintProperty>

### scroll-position
<SlintProperty propName="scroll-position" typeName="length">
The scrolled amount of the content — bind `-flickable.content-y`.
</SlintProperty>

### scroll-release-velocity
<SlintProperty propName="scroll-release-velocity" typeName="float">
`flickable.release-velocity` on the scroll axis — seeds the settle
animation.
</SlintProperty>

### offset / content-offset
<SlintProperty propName="offset" typeName="length">
Current exit offset and accumulated scroll offset (in-out; restore to
preserve state).
</SlintProperty>

### collapsed-fraction
<SlintProperty propName="collapsed-fraction" typeName="float">
0–1 collapse progress combining the exit offset and the clip collapse.
</SlintProperty>

## Callbacks

### fab-clicked()
Invoked when the floating action button is clicked.

### leading-item-clicked(index) / trailing-item-clicked(index)
Invoked when a slot item is clicked.

### leading-item-checked-changed(index, checked) / trailing-item-checked-changed(index, checked)
Invoked when a toggleable slot item's state changes.
