A tooltip displays a descriptive message about its anchor — `TooltipBox` + the `TooltipScope.PlainTooltip`/`TooltipScope.RichTooltip` surfaces in `Tooltip.kt` upstream.

## TooltipBox

`TooltipBox` wraps the anchor content and shows the tooltip above it — below when there is not enough room — centered on the anchor like `rememberTooltipPositionProvider` (plain tooltips), or start-aligned with the upstream rich-tooltip fallback chain when `rich_tooltip` is set. The caret (the 16x8 triangle of `TooltipDefaults.caretSize`) points at the anchor's center while `caret` is enabled.

The tooltip shows when the anchor is hovered, holds keyboard focus, or receives a touch long-press (500ms, `BasicTooltipBox`'s `onLongClick`), matching `TooltipState`'s defaults; non-persistent tooltips dismiss themselves after `TooltipDuration` (1500ms). `persistent` (or `has_action`, which implies it) keeps the tooltip up until dismissed — Escape on the keyboard or `dismissed-by-user` upstream. The enter transition is the M3E `FastSpatial` scale (0.8→1) + `FastEffects` fade, suppressed under `MaterialPalette.reduced-motion`.

### tooltip
<SlintProperty propName="tooltip" typeName="string">
The plain tooltip text — upstream's `tooltip = { PlainTooltip { Text(text) } }` slot content. Empty shows nothing.
</SlintProperty>

### rich_tooltip / rich_title / rich_text / rich_action
<SlintProperty propName="rich_tooltip" typeName="bool" defaultValue="false">
Draw the `RichTooltip` surface instead: `rich_title` fills the upstream `title` slot, `rich_text` the required text slot, and `rich_action` the `action` slot (an `action` also makes the tooltip persistent, as upstream requires).
</SlintProperty>

### preferred_position
<SlintProperty propName="preferred_position" typeName="TooltipAnchorPosition" defaultValue="above">
`above` or `below` — upstream's `rememberPlainTooltipPositionProvider()`/`rememberRichTooltipPositionProvider()` spacing argument; the provider falls back to the other side when the preferred side does not fit the viewport.
</SlintProperty>

### enable_user_input / has_action / persistent
<SlintProperty propName="enable_user_input" typeName="bool" defaultValue="true">
`BasicTooltipBox`'s `enableUserInput` — disables hover/press triggers. `has_action` forces `persistent` and focuses the tooltip's action, matching upstream.
</SlintProperty>

### show_tooltip
<SlintProperty propName="show_tooltip" typeName="bool" defaultValue="false">
Force the tooltip visible without a trigger — the harness/docs equivalent of calling `TooltipState.show()` once.
</SlintProperty>

## PlainTooltip

`PlainTooltip` draws the surface itself (InverseSurface on `CornerExtraSmall`, `InverseOnSurface` `BodySmall`, `sizeIn(40px min, 200px max, 24px min)`, 8px horizontal / 4px vertical padding) — use it standalone for scene/tests, or leave `caret` on and place it over an anchor via `caret_edge`/`caret_x`.

## RichTooltip

`RichTooltip` draws the elevated surface (`SurfaceContainer` on `CornerMedium`, `ElevationTokens.Level2` shadow, `sizeIn` max 320px, 16px horizontal padding): `title` as `TitleSmall` on a 28dp first-baseline, `text` as `BodyMedium`, and `action_text` as a `LabelLarge` action row (`requiredHeightIn(36)`, 8dp bottom padding).

## TooltipContent / TooltipAnchorPosition

`TooltipContent` is the positioned surface `TooltipBox` renders — plain or rich by `rich`, following the provider math over `anchor_abs_x`/`anchor_abs_y`/`anchor_width`/`anchor_height`. `TooltipAnchorPosition` is the `above`/`below` enum, `TooltipCaretEdge` the caret's attached `top`/`bottom` edge.

## ToolTip (deprecated)

`ToolTip` is the pre-M3E name kept as an alias of `PlainTooltip`. The `tooltip:`/`tooltip_offset:` convenience props on touch areas (`FocusTouchArea`, `ExtendedTouchArea`) still work — `tooltip` text shows through the new position provider; `tooltip_offset` is ignored, the provider places the tooltip itself.

## Deviations from upstream

The overlay draws in the window's item tree instead of a real platform `Popup` — a composable tooltip inside `TooltipBox` lives above the anchor's own popup layer upstream, and Slint's `PopupWindow` cannot size-to-content before being positioned. Placement still follows the provider math and clamps against `MaterialWindowAdapter.viewport_width`/`viewport_height`. Carets render as the triangular caret only; upstream's `TooltipCaretShape` container-union outline is not reproduced.
