`FabElevation`

This enum selects the resting elevation of a FAB container — the upstream
`FloatingActionButtonDefaults` elevation factories.

* **`normal`**: `elevation()` — 6px resting, rising on hover.
* **`lowered`**: `loweredElevation()` — 1px resting.
* **`bottom_app_bar`**: `bottomAppBarFabElevation()` — flat inside a bottom app bar.
