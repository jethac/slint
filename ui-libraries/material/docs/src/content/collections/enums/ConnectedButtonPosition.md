`ConnectedButtonPosition`

This enum selects which connected-button-group item shape a `ConnectedButton` takes: `start` is the leading item of a horizontal group (the top item of a vertical one), `end` the trailing (bottom) one.

* **`start`**: Outer corners on the leading (top) edge.
* **`middle`**: Inner corners all around.
* **`end`**: Outer corners on the trailing (bottom) edge.
