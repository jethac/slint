`FabAlignment`

This enum picks the anchor a `shown`/`target_scale` transition grows from
or shrinks toward — the nine `Modifier.animateFloatingActionButton`
alignment points.

* **`top_start`**, **`top_center`**, **`top_end`**
* **`center_start`**, **`center`**, **`center_end`**
* **`bottom_start`**, **`bottom_center`**, **`bottom_end`**
